# Third-party audio

`Resources/RecordedKeys/` contains key-press recordings derived from **Single Key Press Sounds**:

- Copyright 2012 [eklee](http://freesound.org/people/eklee/) (original recordings) and
  [qubodup](http://freesound.org/people/qubodup/) (extraction of single sounds)
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/single-key-press-sounds
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) (text in `Resources/RecordedKeys/LICENSE-CC-BY-3.0.txt`)
- Changes: converted to mono 16-bit, trimmed, faded, level-normalized, split into per-press files.

The six synthesized profiles contain no third-party audio.
